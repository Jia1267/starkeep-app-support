Account and privacy requests

You can manage your profile and request account deletion in the app's Settings. If you cannot access the app, email us from the address associated with your Starkeep account and describe the request. Do not send your password, verification code, or private media in email.

Report a problem

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, the app version shown in TestFlight or Settings, and a brief description of the steps that led to the problem. You may attach a screenshot after hiding names and other private details.
