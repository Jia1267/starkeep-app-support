Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026 · Operator: Jia Guo (郭家), New York, United States · Contact: xingce0789@gmail.com

Who this covers

This policy describes the U.S. V1 of Starkeep, a private, single-person life-achievement journal for adults 18 and older. It is not directed to children. A sign-in account may be created before age confirmation, but recording features remain unavailable to people under 18. They can request account deletion on the age-blocked screen or by email. No automatic deletion period for unused preliminary accounts has been established.

Information processed

To provide the service, Starkeep processes sign-in email and authentication information; nickname and date of birth; achievements, dates, titles, notes, and your own participant information; photos, videos, Live Photos, and audio you choose to add. Supabase also processes connection information such as IP address for authentication security and service logs; the exact provider retention periods remain under review.

Use and sharing

This information is used to operate and secure accounts, save and show your private records, handle uploads and deletions you request, and respond to support. Records are private by default. The U.S. V1 has no public sharing or social feed. Starkeep does not sell personal information, run behavioral ads, or use uploaded media for AI features.

Service providers and backup

Supabase provides authentication, database, and media storage in its us-west-1 region. Apple distributes the iOS app. The operator separately exports the production database, authentication data, and media to an encrypted backup in the operator's Google Drive account. App users are not asked to connect their Google Drive accounts.

Retention and deletion

Your active records remain under your account until you remove an item or request account deletion. Removing the app does not remove cloud records. In-app account deletion starts a server-side process for authentication, database records, and media. Encrypted backup snapshots are configured for up to 30-day retention; a snapshot may still contain data deleted during that period, and deletion requests must be applied again during recovery. Contact us to ask about provider service-log retention.

Your choices

You can review and correct your profile, remove individual content, or request account deletion in Settings. For access, correction, deletion, or privacy questions, contact xingce0789@gmail.com.

Security and changes

Starkeep uses access controls to limit access to private records. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Material policy changes will be communicated in the app and, when required, consent will be requested again.
